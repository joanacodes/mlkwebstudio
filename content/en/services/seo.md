## How we work on SEO

We start with the searches that matter: what a client types when they need you, in your area, today. Each of those searches gets a page that answers it clearly, with the details that make you the obvious choice: your speciality, your area, your proof.

Then the technical side: fast pages, a clean structure, page titles and descriptions, structured data, internal links between related pages, and a sitemap that search engines can read. Nothing clever, just the work done properly and kept up over time.

## A result

For Pilotech’s air conditioning without an outdoor unit, a specialist subject with a precise audience, we built dedicated pages around Teknopoint, the manufacturer whose units it installs. The site now ranks **first or second on Google**.

## What you get

- A list of the searches worth winning, agreed with you.
- New or reworked pages for each of them.
- The technical fixes and foundations your site needs.
- Regular follow-up of positions and enquiries.
