## How we build a site

We start from your business, not from a template: what you sell, to whom, and what a good enquiry looks like. From there we plan the pages your clients actually need, write or rework the copy with you, and design every page for the phone first.

The site is built to be fast and easy to maintain, with clean code, real page titles, structured data and a sitemap, so search engines understand it from the first day. When the business needs more than pages, such as bookings, quotes or a tool for your team, we build a web app alongside it.

## What you get

- A site designed around your offer and your clients’ questions, in French, English or both.
- Pages that load fast and read well on any phone.
- The technical groundwork for SEO: titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap, redirects.
- Contact and quote forms that reach your inbox.
- A handover you can live with: you can edit the content, or we keep looking after it.
