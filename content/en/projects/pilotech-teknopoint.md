## The brief

Pilotech, an installer based in Sucy-en-Brie, works across Paris and the Île-de-France. Its Teknopoint line is a rare speciality: air conditioning with no outdoor unit, from the Italian manufacturer Teknopoint. It suits buildings where a façade unit is not an option: Haussmann-era and listed buildings, protected areas, strict co-ownerships.

The people who need it search in their own words: air conditioning without an outdoor unit, invisible air conditioning. Pilotech had to be the answer they find. The other opportunity was business premises in Paris that need discreet cooling, which Pilotech had never approached.

## What we did

Each Teknopoint range has its own page: ELFO, IDRA NEXT, IDRA NEXT RING, IDRA ECO, MOKA and PICCOLO, in single-room and multi-room versions where both exist. The pages show what each range leaves visible from the street, from two small grilles for ELFO to nothing at all for IDRA NEXT, and their titles and addresses carry the words people search for. The blog, worked on for the same searches, answers the questions asked before buying, such as how to hide an air conditioner.

For businesses, our cold email practice, MLK Leadhunters, set up dedicated sending addresses and wrote short sequences for three audiences: Paris restaurants, shops and offices, and the architects who specify air conditioning for their clients’ projects. Three short plain-text emails at most, no links, an invitation to call and to look at the catalogue on the website, around one offer: a free technical visit and a firm quote, for an installation invisible from the street. The emails were signed by the founder, with his phone number in the email.

## The result

Pilotech Teknopoint now ranks **first or second on Google**, and the site does the rest: a visitor looking for air conditioning without an outdoor unit can find the range that suits their building and ask for a quote.

The emails also did something we had not planned: many prospects, architects especially, called instead of replying. A call fits a busy day better than a written answer, so we made calling even easier in the sequences, and the written replies went straight to Pilotech’s founder, who answered them from his own inbox.
