## The brief

Ginov provides building services for businesses in Paris and Île-de-France, with a speciality in restaurants. Its team takes on electrical work and plumbing, fits air conditioning, maintains offices, shops, restaurants and kitchens, and handles facility management, planning works with the client’s site manager. For restaurants, it also offers dishwashing water treatment and water softeners.

Its clients are professionals: restaurant owners, shop and office managers. They need to see quickly that one company can handle the trade they need, that it works in their area, and how to reach it.

## What we did

We built a website that presents Ginov as **a single contact for the technical side of a business**. Each trade has its place: electrical installations carried out by certified electricians, the installation, repair and upkeep of plumbing networks, regular and responsive maintenance to anticipate breakdowns and keep downtime low, and facility management, with works planned according to urgency and budget.

The site also brings out what sets Ginov apart. The restaurant specialities, dishwashing water treatment and water softeners, show that it knows professional kitchens. And **air conditioning without an outdoor unit** is presented with the arguments that matter in Paris: no application to the town hall or the building’s managing agent, and a way to cool premises such as a basement kitchen.

The homepage names places where the team works, including Paris, Boulogne, Créteil, Colombes and Versailles. The site offers a quote request and brings together the ways to get in touch: phone, email and WhatsApp.

## The result

A restaurant owner or business manager in Île-de-France can now find the trade they need on a single site, from electrical work to air conditioning for a kitchen, and ask Ginov for a quote straight away.
