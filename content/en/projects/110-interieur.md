## The brief

110% Intérieur is an interior architecture and decoration studio in the Paris area. It belongs to the 110% group, which covers property search, renovation and interior design. The studio offers a one-hour advice session to make the most of a space and guide the choice of colours and materials; the Book, with a mood board, a selection of furniture and decoration, colours for walls and floors and a 3D view; a turnkey service; and decoration coaching, which includes visits to shops chosen to suit the client’s taste and budget.

Interior design is a visual trade: people want to see a studio’s style before they book a first session. Social media is a natural place to show it.

## What we did

Our work for the studio focused on social media. We set up its publishing there, to give **its work a showcase** in front of people thinking about redecorating or renovating their home, and to point them towards the services set out on its website.

## The result

The studio’s social media publishing is now set up. Someone looking for ideas for their home can discover its work there, then find on 110interieur.com the service that suits them, from a one-hour advice session to a turnkey project.
