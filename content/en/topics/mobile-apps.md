A good app can still go unnoticed. These guides cover app-store optimisation (the SEO of the App Store and Google Play), the website that supports the app, Apple Ads and Google's App campaigns, and the security and privacy work that comes before launch: API keys, database rules, privacy labels and the obligations of the EU Cyber Resilience Act.
