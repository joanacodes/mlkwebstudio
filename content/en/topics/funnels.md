For a service business, a funnel is simply the path from a first visit, an email or a referral to a booked call and a signed quote. These guides show how to build it with a website, a booking page and an email tool, how to grow and write a newsletter that people read, the consent and deliverability rules to respect, and how to see which channel actually brings clients.
