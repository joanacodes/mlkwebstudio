Cold emailing is the channel that brings conversations fastest, and the one most often done badly. These guides cover what is legal in the UK and in France, the sending infrastructure (domains, mailboxes, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, warm-up), tools such as Instantly, Reoon and Outscraper, and the copy that gets people to reply or call. When you would rather have it run for you, MLK Leadhunters, the studio’s cold emailing practice, does exactly that.
