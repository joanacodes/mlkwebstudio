You do not need an IT department to be hard to attack. A password manager, two-factor authentication, tested backups, a rule for any change of bank details and a one-page incident plan stop most of what hits small teams. These guides cover each of them step by step, with the official guidance from the NCSC and the ANSSI, and the obligations that may reach you through your clients.
