AIO and GEO, optimisation for AI answers, are the new layer on top of SEO. These guides explain how assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews find, trust and quote sources, and the practical changes that make a small business easier to recommend: clear answers, structured data, mentions in the press, llms.txt and the files that let AI crawlers read your site.
