Most of the GDPR comes down to a handful of habits: say what you collect and why, ask before tracking, sign the right contracts, answer requests on time and react fast when something leaks. These guides explain each rule in plain words, with the article of the law and the regulator's own guidance, for EU businesses and for companies in the UK, Switzerland, North America or elsewhere that have users or buyers in the EU.
