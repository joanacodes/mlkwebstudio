Domains, hosting and email are the plumbing of a business online, and most emergencies start there. These tutorials explain the difference between a domain, hosting and an email service, how DNS records work (including the trailing dot that trips everyone up), and how to set things up step by step with Spaceship, Namecheap, OVH, Hostinger, Infomaniak and Squarespace, with honest opinions on which providers suit a small business.
