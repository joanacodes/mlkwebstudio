AI and automation are worth it when they remove a task you do every day. These guides compare assistants for business writing and research, explain AI APIs (keys, costs, first requests), and show automations built with tools such as Zapier, Make, n8n or Google Apps Script, down to the custom internal app that became necessary when a client received more leads than the team could follow.
